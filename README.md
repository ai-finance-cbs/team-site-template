# Team Site Template

Starter for your group's website in B8403 Finance in the Age of AI. The full instructions are on the Milestone #3 page of the course site.

1. Click **Use this template**, then **Create a new repository**. Choose your own account, give it a name, and make it **Public**.
2. In your new repository, open **Settings → Pages**. Under **Build and deployment**, set **Source** to *Deploy from a branch*, choose **main** and **/ (root)**, and click **Save**.
3. After a minute or two, your site is live at `https://<your-username>.github.io/<repository-name>/`.
4. Add your teammates in **Settings → Collaborators**.

Put CVs, photos, and notes in the `private/` folder. Nothing in it is uploaded.
